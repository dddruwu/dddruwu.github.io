LEANR

A body fat and weight tracker for Android. LEANR turns whatever you measure into one body fat number, with the range to go with it.

Get it on Google Play

What the app does

  • Four ways to measure, one resultCalipers, tape, BMI and AI photos. Use one method or all four, and LEANR combines them into a single number.
  • An honest rangeMore methods give a tighter range. When two methods disagree, LEANR tells you which one to doubt and why.
  • Daily weight, done properlyLog your weight in seconds. LEANR smooths out the daily bounce so you see the real trend.
  • Built for the habitA daily weigh-in reminder, a home-screen widget, and backdating for entries you missed.
  • LEANR ProA weekly coach's read, deeper charts, CSV export and no ads, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan.

Your data stays on your phone

There is no account and no login. Your weights, measurements and photos live on your device. Two AI features, the AI photo estimate and the weekly read, send some of your data to our server and on to OpenAI for a few seconds, and only when you use them. It is then discarded. The Privacy Policy has the full details.

LEANR is a fitness tracker, not a medical tool. Estimates can be wrong and are not medical advice.
