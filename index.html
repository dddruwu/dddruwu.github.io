What the app does

Build your own routines
Set up exercises, bell weights, sets and reps the way you actually train.
Train on the clock
Run EMOM, AMRAP, timed and interval work with large on-screen timers and rest countdowns.
Track tonnage and records
Every session totals the weight you moved, and personal records are picked up automatically.
Look back at your history
A calendar shows every session you've logged, with details you can review and edit.
Start with free programs
Built-in beginner programs give you a structured starting point without building anything.
Share your work
Turn a finished session into a share card with your numbers and any new records.

Accounts and your data

You can use Iron Commissar without an account. If you sign in with Google, the app receives your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account to identify your account, so your training history can be backed up and synced across devices.

Iron Commissar does not sell your data. The privacy policy explains what is collected, how it is used, and how to delete your account and data.
