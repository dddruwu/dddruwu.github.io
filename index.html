About

Hi, I'm Andrew.

I'm a senior business systems analyst in Sydney with over 10 years across transport, logistics, retail, media and utilities. I've led system go-lives, set up EDI and integrations with partners, and supported SAP and the operational systems businesses run on every day.

I also design, build and ship Android apps on my own. I write the requirements first, then build with AI coding tools (Claude Code, React Native and Expo), review every change and test on real devices.

Iron Commissar came from my kettlebell training. It went from idea to Google Play in three months and has passed 10,000 downloads with a 4.25-star rating. LEANR came from tracking my own body composition and is now live too.

Away from the screen I'm a husband and a dad. I train with kettlebells most days, and I unwind with the odd game on my Nintendo Switch 2.

I'm open to roles, freelance work and collaborations, so feel free to get in touch.
