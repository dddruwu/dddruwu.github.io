About Andrew Alvaran

Hi, I'm Andrew.

I'm a Sydney-based developer building Android apps on my own. dddruwu is the name I publish under.

I build the tools I want to use myself, then clean them up and release them. Iron Commissar came from my own kettlebell training, and LEANR from tracking my own body composition.

By day I work as a business systems analyst, so I care about apps that are simple, honest about your data and easy to delete.
