What the app does

  • Build your own routinesSet up exercises, bell weights, sets and reps the way you actually train.
  • Train on the clockRun EMOM, AMRAP, timed and interval work with large on-screen timers and rest countdowns.
  • Track tonnage and recordsEvery session totals the weight you moved, and personal records are picked up automatically.
  • Look back at your historyA calendar shows every session you've logged, with details you can review and edit.
  • Start with free programsBuilt-in beginner programs give you a structured starting point without building anything.

Sign in with Google

Iron Commissar uses Google Sign-In, handled through Supabase Authentication, only to create and identify your account. The app receives your name, email address and profile photo from your Google account for that purpose, so your training history can be backed up and synced across devices. You can also use the app without signing in. Iron Commissar does not sell your data.
