Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Iron Commissar ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the App.

Iron Commissar is a hardstyle kettlebell training app for Android. The App is developed and maintained by an individual developer ("the Developer") based in Australia.

1Information We Collect

Account Information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account via Supabase Authentication. This is used to create and identify your account and is not used for any other purpose. Google Sign-In only runs when you choose to sign in.

If you do not sign in, the App may operate in a limited local-only mode without an account, depending on app version.

If you use the App without signing in (as a guest), your data is stored only on your phone and is never uploaded to our servers.

Training Data. To provide the App's core functionality, we collect and store the training data you create, including:

  • Workout routines and routine structure
  • Workout sessions and session details (exercises, sets, reps, timing, free-text session notes and an effort rating)
  • Exercise logs across training protocols (grinds, ballistics, mobility, complexes)
  • Personal records
  • Program progress and trophies
  • Custom exercises and custom kettlebell inventory you add

This data is stored locally on your device and, if you are signed in, securely synced to our cloud database (Supabase) so it is available across your devices.

Photos you choose for a share card are used on your device only and are never uploaded to our servers.

Your bodyweight setting and your kg/lb preference stay on your phone. Sets you log for bodyweight exercises store the weight used, and session totals and personal record values calculated from it are also stored. If you are signed in, these are backed up to your account. Weights are always uploaded in kilograms. Other settings, such as date format, reminders and timer sound, stay on your phone.

Advertising Data. The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including banner and full-screen interstitial ads. AdMob may collect and process data such as your advertising ID (AdID), device information, and general usage data to serve and measure ads. Where required by law (including for users in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland), we request your consent before personalized ads are shown, via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The App shows a Google consent form first, and whether ads are personalized depends on the consent you give. Google may receive your advertising ID (subject to your consent choice and your Android ad settings), your IP address and device information to serve ads. The Google ads library also declares Android's ad services permissions (ad ID, attribution and topics). Google may use them for ads, subject to your consent choice and Android's ad settings.

For more information on how Google uses this data, see Google's Privacy & Terms and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Purchase Information. If you make a purchase (such as removing ads), purchase and subscription status is processed by RevenueCat and linked to your account identifier. For users without an account, purchases are linked to a random identifier generated on your device, not an account identifier. If you use the App as a guest, your purchase record held by our purchases provider (RevenueCat) is identified only by a random ID. It is not deleted by the App. We do not directly receive or store your payment card details — these are handled by Google Play's billing system.

Information We Do Not Collect. The App has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK of its own. The third-party services listed in this policy are Google Sign-In, Supabase, Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads), RevenueCat, Google Play Billing and Google's Firebase messaging component. Of these, the Google components (Google Sign-In, Google AdMob, Google Play Billing and the Firebase messaging component) may store technical event or diagnostic data on your device, as described by Google. We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or other device data beyond what is described in this policy.

2How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core training and tracking features
  • Sync your data across your devices when signed in
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Process purchases and manage premium features
  • Maintain and improve the App

We do not sell your personal information.

4Data Retention and Deletion

Retention

Your training data is retained for as long as your account is active.

If you are signed in

In versions of the app that include Settings > Delete account, go to Settings, tap Delete account and confirm. In those versions, deletion on our servers is immediate.

If you are signed in, it permanently deletes your cloud data (your workout sessions and the logs inside them, personal records, program progress and trophies, routines and their blocks, complex templates, kettlebells and custom exercises), also asks our subscription provider, RevenueCat, to delete your purchase record, and then deletes your sign-in account.

On your phone

In those versions the app also removes your workout data, settings, trophies, scheduled notifications and the photos or files it created on your device (the exported history file and temporary image files) from your phone. This clean-up removes the data of the account being deleted.

In one rare case, if you first used the app as a guest and then signed in, some records from the guest period that could not be merged into your account can stay on that phone until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. We plan to make the app remove these as well in a future update.

Deleting your account on our servers needs an internet connection. If the connection drops, the phone clean-up finishes the next time the app opens with a connection.

Your other devices

Your other devices are signed out shortly after the account is deleted, when the app next refreshes its sign-in. Data already saved on them stays there until you erase it on that device or uninstall the app.

What stays on your phone

After you delete your account, these stay on your phone until you uninstall the app or clear its storage: the technical installation identifier created by the Firebase component, the app's ad and review pacing counters, your Google consent choice and Google's own technical event store.

Older versions

In older versions of the app, the "Erase all data" button removes much of your data from your device and our cloud database, but does not remove everything: it does not remove your sign-in account, program progress, trophies, or some other server records. If you use an older version, email us to have your account and data deleted, or update the app if a version that includes Settings > Delete account is available for your device.

Guests

If you use the App as a guest, your data is stored only on your phone, so we cannot find it by email. In versions of the app that include Settings > Delete account, use "Delete my data" in the app. In any version, you can also uninstall the app. Your purchase record, identified only by a random ID, is not deleted by the app.

Email requests

To request deletion by email, email us at the address below from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Iron Commissar account". We may need to confirm that the request really comes from that account. We complete email deletion requests within 30 days. When we process an emailed request, we also delete your RevenueCat purchase record. Records held by Google, such as Google Play purchase records and Google's advertising services, are managed by Google and are not deleted by us. Full instructions are at https://dddruwu.com/iron-commissar/delete-account.html.

We do not keep our own backups of your data. Our database provider may keep short-lived technical logs and infrastructure copies that we cannot access, and these are removed on the provider's own schedule.

Uninstalling the App removes locally stored data from your device but does not delete data already synced to our cloud database. If you have uninstalled the App, follow Option 2 on the deletion page linked above to request deletion.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have rights under the GDPR and equivalent regulations, including the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time via the App's "Manage consent" option in Settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us at the email address below.

6Children's Privacy

The App is rated for general audiences but is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including using Supabase's row-level security to ensure your data is only accessible to your authenticated account. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.
