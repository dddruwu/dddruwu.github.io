Delete Account

There are two ways to delete your data: from inside the app, or by email. Which in-app button you have depends on your version of the app.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

In versions of the app that include Settings > Delete account:

  1. Open the Iron Commissar app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm when asked.

In those versions, deletion on our servers is immediate. Deleting your account on our servers needs an internet connection. If the connection drops, the phone clean-up finishes the next time the app opens with a connection. Your other devices will be signed out the next time they connect. Data already saved on them stays there until you erase it on that device or uninstall the app.

In older versions of the app, the "Erase all data" button removes much of your data from your device and our cloud database, but does not remove everything: it does not remove your sign-in account, program progress, trophies, or some other server records. If you use an older version, email us to have your account and data deleted, or update the app if a version that includes Settings > Delete account is available for your device.

Option 2: Request full account deletion

Use this if you use an older version of the app that only has Erase all data, or if you have uninstalled the app. Email dddruwu@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Iron Commissar account. Send it from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, so we can match the request to your account. We may need to confirm that the request really comes from that account.

We complete email deletion requests within 30 days. When we process an emailed request, we also delete your RevenueCat purchase record.

If you use the App as a guest, your data is stored only on your phone, so we cannot find it by email. In versions of the app that include Settings > Delete account, use "Delete my data" in the app. In any version, you can also uninstall the app.

What gets deleted

In versions of the app that include Settings > Delete account, from our cloud database:

  • Your workout sessions and the logs inside them
  • Personal records
  • Program progress and trophies
  • Routines and their blocks
  • Complex templates
  • Kettlebells
  • Custom exercises

In those versions, if you are signed in, it also asks our subscription provider, RevenueCat, to delete your purchase record, and then deletes your sign-in account (your email address, name and profile photo, held by Supabase).

In those versions, the app removes your workout data, settings, trophies, scheduled notifications and the photos or files it created on your device (the exported history file and temporary image files) from your phone.

If you email us, we delete your data, your sign-in account and your RevenueCat purchase record.

What is not deleted

  • Records held by Google, such as Google Play purchase records and Google's advertising services, are managed by Google and are not deleted by us.
  • If you use the app as a guest, your purchase record, identified only by a random ID, is not deleted by the app.
  • Data already saved on your other devices stays there until you erase it on that device or uninstall the app.

We do not keep our own backups of your data. Our database provider may keep short-lived technical logs and infrastructure copies that we cannot access, and these are removed on the provider's own schedule.
