Delete Account

There are two ways to delete your data. The in-app button erases your workout data. Removing your Google sign-in account as well needs a request by email.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the Iron Commissar app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm in the two pop-ups that appear.

The app then erases all your workout data from your phone and from our cloud database, and signs you out. This button does not remove your sign-in account (the Google sign-in record held by our authentication provider, Supabase). To remove that too, use Option 2.

Option 2: Request full account deletion

Use this if you have uninstalled the app, or if you want your sign-in account removed as well. Email dddruwu@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Iron Commissar account. Send it from the email address of the Google account you signed in with, so we can match the request to your account.

We will delete your account data within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

What gets deleted

Workout data (Option 1 or Option 2), from your phone and from our cloud database:

  • Workout history
  • Routines
  • Kettlebells
  • Custom exercises
  • Personal records

Sign-in account (Option 2 only), the Google sign-in record held by Supabase:

  • Email address
  • Name
  • Profile photo
